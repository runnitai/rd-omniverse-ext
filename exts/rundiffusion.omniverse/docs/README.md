# RunDiffusion for Omniverse

Generate AI images from your viewport and add generated 3D assets directly to
your stage, all from a panel inside your Omniverse application.

RunDiffusion connects your scene to its creative tool catalogue so you can
capture a view, choose a tool, and generate while you continue working. Compare
images with your original capture, reuse results for another generation, or
bring a generated 3D asset into your scene.

## What you can do

- **Capture the view you want.** Preview your viewport live, then select
  Capture view to lock in the image you will send. You can also capture from a
  camera already set up in your scene. Your captured image stays fixed as you
  continue moving around.
- **Find tools visually.** Browse tools by thumbnail, explore kits, or search
  the catalogue. Each tool displays its supported settings.
- **See costs before generating.** The cost updates as you adjust your
  settings. The Billed to account stays visible beside Generate, so you can
  check which account will pay.
- **Keep working during generation.** Runs process in the background while your
  viewport continues rendering. Track their progress and find completed results
  in the Library tab.
- **Use results in your workflow.** Compare an image with its source capture,
  select Use in Create to make it your next input, or choose Add to stage for a
  generated 3D asset. Assets are placed and selected, and you can undo the
  addition.
- **Arrange your workspace.** Dock the RunDiffusion panel beside your tools, or
  open Library and Uploads in separate windows. Whether you left each one open
  is remembered for next time.

Your scene changes only when you choose to make a change.

## Saving your work

Results listed under This session are temporary and are not automatically
stored in your account library. Select Save or Send to RunDiffusion to keep
them. Hiding a generation only removes it from the run list; it does not cancel
the run or reverse its charge.

Beta. Installation instructions came with your download, and the version you
are running is on the Account tab.
